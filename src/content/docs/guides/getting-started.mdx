## Setup Instructions

<Steps>

1. **Access Your A4B Platform**
   Log in to your A4B account and navigate to the platform dashboard. Verify that all required modules are active and accessible.

2. **Initial Configuration**
   Configure user roles, permissions, and initial workflows. Integrate with existing enterprise systems like CRM, ERP, or HR platforms.

3. **Onboarding and Training**
   Utilize A4B's onboarding tutorials and documentation to train your team. Ensure all stakeholders are familiar with the platform's features and functionality.

</Steps>

## Maintenance and Updates
